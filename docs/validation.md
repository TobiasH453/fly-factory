# Brain validation

Generated by `tools/build_connectome.py`. All simulations use the Shiu et al. 2024 LIF model (dt 0.1 ms, 1 s trials) on the FlyWire v783 connectome.

- Full brain: **138,639 neurons, 15,091,983 connections**
- Shipped activity-pruned subnetwork: **5,147 neurons, 381,657 connections (3,015,835 synapses)**
- Identical-seed runs, subnetwork vs full brain, every neuron's spike count: **exact match**
- Held-out seeds/rates, worst readout-population deviation: **2.30 Hz**

## Notes

- Bitter GRNs at 150 Hz can stochastically ignite a runaway loop through SMP/MB dopaminergic neurons (SMP108, SMP177, CRE011, PPL107 ...) that recruits >10k neurons. The game therefore caps every input at the envelope above (sugar 150 Hz, bitter 100 Hz, dust 80 Hz, wind 120 Hz, hearing 150 Hz, loomL 150 Hz, loomR 150 Hz), inside which no condition ignited.
- Grooming DNs are selected from data: Descending neurons selected by response to head mechanosensation (dust/wind) and not to taste or looming: DNg15, DNg84, DNg85, DNge036.
