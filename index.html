🪰 Fly Factory Tycoon

You're the boss. Your one employee is a fruit fly, and its brain is a real connectome simulation: the FlyWire whole-brain wiring diagram running the leaky integrate-and-fire model of Shiu et al. (2024) live in your browser.

  • Game logic walks it between the egg tray and the incubator. Every hatched fly pays out.
  • Its neurons decide when it eats (sugar GRNs → MN9), grooms (dust on bristles → grooming DNs), escapes (looming → LC4/LPLC2 → Giant Fiber) and turns away from threats (DNa02).
  • Feed it 🍬 when it's tired, blow 💨 the dust off, spray 🧪 bitter to stop a snack, or swat 🗞️ it. Rewire it in the 🔬 Opto Lab.

Spike timing is stochastic (Poisson sensory input), so the worker never reacts exactly the same way twice. The status badge shows whether the current action is neural or scripted.
